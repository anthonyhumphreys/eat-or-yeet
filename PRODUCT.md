# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People checking packaged food at home or while shopping, usually on a phone and often with limited time. Their primary task is to capture identifying information from a product, compare it with current official warnings, and understand why a result was returned.

## Product purpose

Eat or Yeet checks barcodes and visible packaging information against a structured set of current food-safety incidents. It gives a conservative, explainable verdict without claiming that an unmatched product is safe. Success means a user can reach a useful, source-backed result in under a minute and can tell what evidence produced it.

## Positioning

A barcode and manual recall checker. Deterministic rules compare label details with official warnings and explain possible matches. The app uses no AI or photo uploads.

## Brand personality

Fast, cheeky, and credible. The joke belongs in the name and supporting copy. Safety advice stays calm, precise, and prominent.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, medical alarmism, novelty meme sites, full-screen traffic-light colour, fake certainty, decorative AI imagery, and interfaces that bury official advice behind playful copy.

## Design principles

- Put barcode scanning and manual entry within immediate reach.
- Keep the first step short; ask for batch or date details when they matter.
- Call an unmatched result "No matching warning found" and show its coverage and check time.
- Make verdicts legible in a cropped screenshot without using a "safe" badge.
- Explain every warning with the exact matched evidence and source.
- Preserve uncertainty. Missing evidence produces NO CLUE, not reassurance.
- Import official warnings automatically so the public flow stays current without manual curation.

## Accessibility & inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on colour alone. Support keyboard navigation, clear focus states, screen readers, reduced motion, high text contrast, iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, desktop browsers, and camera-permission fallbacks.
