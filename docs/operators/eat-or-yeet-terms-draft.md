## What a check means

Eat or Yeet compares the barcode and packet details you supply with published food warnings from the UK or US sources shown in your result. Choose the country relevant to your product and give only details you know. Leave unknown details empty.

A result showing no matching warning does not prove that food is safe, that it has not been recalled, or that a warning does not exist elsewhere. Results depend on the details supplied and the available source data. Coverage can be incomplete or out of date. A failed check or missing source coverage is inconclusive.

Read any related official notice and compare the affected product, batch, dates and other details with your packet. Follow the advice in the official notice. Eat or Yeet does not replace advice from a food-safety authority, manufacturer or retailer.

Eat or Yeet does not provide medical advice, diagnoses or treatment recommendations.

## AI assistants and Notion

An AI assistant may send the product details you provide to Eat or Yeet and summarize the returned information. Check the official source links rather than relying only on the assistant's wording. Your AI provider's terms and privacy policy apply to your use of that provider.

The ChatGPT plugin and other public AI connections provide checks on request. They do not continuously monitor products or create future recall alerts.

When automatic Notion reviews are available and you connect a list, Eat or Yeet reviews configured items when they change and writes results into the dedicated review fields. Those reviews do not monitor for new recalls after the item has been checked. Copying the Notion template alone does not connect your workspace or enable automatic reviews.

Only connect a Notion workspace and list you are authorized to manage. You can pause reviews or disconnect through the management page. Disconnecting does not delete your shopping list from Notion.

Service limits, maintenance and failures can prevent a check from completing. Use the relevant official source directly when Eat or Yeet is unavailable or a result is inconclusive.
